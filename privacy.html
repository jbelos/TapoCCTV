Privacy policy

TapoCCTV is a personal camera recorder operated by its owner on their own Windows PC. This notice describes the recorder and its optional Google Drive connection.

Camera data on the PC

The recorder receives camera video, audio and motion events over the owner's local network. It stores recording files and camera/date history on that PC. Its dashboard and live feeds are accessible on the recorder PC through its local loopback address.

Google Drive data

With the owner's consent, TapoCCTV uses Google's drive.file scope. It uses authorized recording files and their metadata, including file names, folders, IDs, sizes and checksums, to place clips in the intended folder, verify uploads, retrieve clips for playback and apply the owner's retention settings. It also checks the account's storage quota. The recorder does not request full Drive access.

Storage and sharing

Recordings are copied to the owner's Google Drive. Google stores them under the account's sharing settings. The recorder verifies its destination and does not create public sharing links. It does not transmit footage to an app-operated server, sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

The recorder keeps authorization material, upload receipts and motion history on the owner's PC in files restricted by Windows permissions. A selected recording may be temporarily cached on that PC for playback. These informational pages contain no camera footage, camera links or sign-in form.

Retention and your choices

Unuploaded footage is kept for retry. The recorder removes temporary originals only after verified upload. Under the default 30-day cloud setting it moves eligible old clips to Google Drive Trash, where Google's own deletion policy applies. The owner can change this setting, manage recordings in Drive and remove local project files.

The owner can revoke the app's Drive authorization from their Google Account's third-party connections. Revoking access stops the recorder's Drive operations; existing files stay under the owner's control in Drive.

Contact

For questions about this personal app or its Google authorization, use the support contact displayed in the Google consent screen.
