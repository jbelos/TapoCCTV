Privacy policy

Independent personal project

A private recorder for your own cameras.

TapoCCTV records local camera video and audio to a Windows PC. The owner can authorize the app to keep verified recordings in their own Google Drive.

  1. 01Local cameras

    Video and motion events travel on the owner's home network.

  2. 02Windows recorder

    Continuous clips and motion markers are available in a dashboard on that PC.

  3. 03Private Google Drive

    Completed clips are copied to the owner's folder and checked before local cleanup.

Why the app requests Drive access

TapoCCTV uses the Google Drive drive.file permission to upload, verify, retrieve and manage the retention of its authorized recording files. Google sign-in and consent take place on the owner's device.
